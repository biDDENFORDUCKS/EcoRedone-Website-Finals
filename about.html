ABOUT US

Eco-Re-Done is a collaborative project created by a group of students from the
Introduction to Computing (Web Design and Development) course. This website was
developed as part of our graded project requirement, under the guidance and facilitation
of our instructor Prof. Arsenio Arellano.

Our goal is to design an educational website that promotes proper waste management,
encourages environmental awareness, and teaches users simple ways to reuse, recycle,
and transform waste into useful or profitable items. Through this project, we aimed to
apply our knowledge in HTML, CSS, website planning, and content developers.
