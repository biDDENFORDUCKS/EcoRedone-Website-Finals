MISSION & VISION

Our Mission

To create an easy-to-understand, visually appealing, and informative platform that helps
users learn the difference between biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste, and
inspires small actions that benefit the environment.

Our Vision

A community where individuals — especially students — practice responsible waste
management and actively contribute to a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable future.
