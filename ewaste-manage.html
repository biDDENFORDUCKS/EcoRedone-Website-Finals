HOW TO MANAGE E‑WASTE

Proper E‑waste management protects the environment and human health:

    Prevents toxic contamination
    Reduces landfill load
    Conserves valuable metals
    Protects water and soil
    Encourages sustainable recycling
    Separate electronic devices from general waste
    Donate or sell functional electronics
    Recycle batteries, phones, and computers responsibly
    Use certified e‑waste recycling centers
    Avoid illegal dumping of electronics
