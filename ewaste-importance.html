E-WASTE IS A GROWING PROBLEM

Because electronic devices contain toxic materials, improper disposal can:

  • Contaminate soil and water
  • Release hazardous chemicals
  • Harm humans and wildlife
  • Waste valuable materials like gold and copper
  • Increase landfill burden
  • Contribute to climate change
