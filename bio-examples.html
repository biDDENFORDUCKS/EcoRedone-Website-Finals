Biodegradable Waste Examples

Food Waste

  • Fruit peels
  • Vegetable scraps
  • Eggshells
  • Leftover rice or bread
  • Coffee grounds and tea bags

Paper-Based Materials

  • Newspapers
  • Cardboard
  • Paper bags
  • Tissue paper (unused or lightly used)

Garden and Plant Waste

  • Leaves
  • Grass clippings
  • Branches
  • Flowers
  • Fruit seeds

Natural Materials

  • Wood
  • Cotton
  • Jute
  • Bamboo
  • Coconut husk

By handling biodegradable waste properly, we reduce our carbon footprint and support the natural recycling process of the Earth.
