TIN CAN PENCIL HOLDER
Materials: Tin can, glue, paper, string, paint

How to make:

1. Remove or smooth any sharp edges
2. Decorate with paint, colored paper, or fabric
3. Add string, stickers, or designs
4. Use as a pencil or brush holder
